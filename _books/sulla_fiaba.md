_Sulla fiaba_ gave me a set of useful tools for approaching folktales more attentively. In particular, Calvino helped me look for recurring structures and character types, while also paying attention to the editorial choices that shape how these stories reach the reader. It did not make me see every tale as part of a single rigid system; rather, it showed me some of the underlying properties I might otherwise have overlooked.

The style is more academic than Calvino's novels, but it remains clear. I found the essays interesting and, above all, valuable as preparation for reading _Fiabe italiane_: they gave me a framework I can now use without prescribing what I should find.

I would not recommend _Sulla fiaba_ universally. It is a specialized book, best suited to readers who are already interested in folktales and want to examine them with greater awareness.
