This book was originally gifted to me by a friend shortly after my wedding, because at the time (August 2020) the plan was to spend the honeymoon in Australia and New Zeleand. COVID-19 had other plans for us unfortunately, so that didn't end up happening. This, however, led to us having an _unforgettable_ honeymoon in Hawaii, a truly unique trip.

This book made me discover a land I knew very little about, throught the eyes of its indigenous people. Before reading this book I had no idea what the Songlines were, or the relationship between the australian indigenous people and their land, and I am now more eager than ever to visit Australia.

The book was a fascinating glimpse into a different part of the world and a different way of being human, as the book contains lots of fascinating reflections and anthopological insights into travelling and migrating. 

Chatwin's writing style is very vivid: he can very clearly portrait characters with very few paragraphs, and can describe scenes he witnessed with a visual clarity that makes it very easy to picture them, as though you were witnessing them with him.
