I’m **Pinco Pallino**, a Senior Data Scientist at Merkle Italy. I start from a problem someone needs to solve, then design, build, deploy, and maintain the application, and keep improving it with the people who use it.

## Background

Before moving into industry, I completed a PhD in physics at the University of Padua, with a visiting fellowship at Harvard’s Physics Department, and spent four years doing experimental biophysics research at UC San Diego, where I won a competitive three-year fellowship from the Simons Foundation.

Experiments taught me to question how data was collected and how much a result can actually tell me. I still look for weak assumptions, and I change the plan when the evidence says so.

## What I work on

- LLM and agentic applications: retrieval, evaluation, and deployment on GCP.
- Analytics and decision support: turning ambiguous questions into testable analyses.
- Geospatial analysis and data products.

## Roles and education

- Senior Data Scientist, Merkle Italy
- Postdoctoral researcher in experimental biophysics, UC San Diego (four years)
- PhD in Physics, University of Padua
- Visiting fellowship, Harvard Physics Department
