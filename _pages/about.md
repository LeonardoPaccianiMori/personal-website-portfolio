# About Me

I'm a Senior Data Scientist at [Merkle](https://www.merkle.com/en.html) Italy, where I work in the space between data, models, and business decisions in the martech world.

Before moving into industry I worked in academia doing research in biophysics, with experience at UC San Diego and Harvard. That background still shapes how I approach data science: I care about evidence, uncertainty, experimental design, and whether an analysis can survive contact with messy real-world data.

What I enjoy most (professionally speaking) is turning ambiguous questions into useful systems and information. Sometimes that means building a model, sometimes designing a custom algorithm, a better dataset, a clearer dashboard, a more reliable way to translate technical results into decisions, sometimes a mixture of them.

This site collects projects I've worked on that reflect how I think through data problems: practically, carefully, and with a bias toward work that can *actually* be used.
